# Rule 10: Product Strategy, Feature Innovation & UX Refinement Standards

## 1. Autonomous Product Leadership Mandate
* The Founder is not expected to micro-manage feature requirements or perform competitive product research.
* The **Product Strategy & Innovation Department** is responsible for independently researching, detailing, and refining product features into market-leading, enterprise-grade AI SaaS capabilities.

## 2. World-Class E-Commerce AI Feature Standards
Every feature designed for **ShopAgent** must meet the **"Elite In-Store Salesperson"** standard:
1. **Context-Aware Recommendations**:
   * Never output generic lists. Explain *why* a product matches the shopper's specific context (e.g. "For daily road running under 10km, this dual Air Zoom cushioning offers better impact protection").
2. **Proactive Sales Intelligence**:
   * Identify complementary items (e.g. matching trail running socks with trail shoes).
   * Provide transparent, honest trade-offs between competing models (e.g. Pegasus vs Ultraboost cushioning vs weight).
3. **Frictionless Action Dispatching**:
   * Every conversational decision must offer an immediate one-click UI action (e.g. `[Compare Specs]`, `[Select Size 10]`, `[Add to Cart]`).

## 3. Product Roadmap Refinement Protocol
Before any milestone begins:
* Refine high-level goals into concrete shopper user journeys and merchant value metrics.
* Eliminate low-impact or confusing UI flows.
* Guarantee that merchant setup requires zero engineering effort (<5 minute embed).
